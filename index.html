01 — Get to Know Me!

Design shaped by two ways of seeing.

I'm an emerging architect with a strong passion for sustainable and human-centered design. My journey in architecture is shaped not only by my academic work but also by my background in photography, which has sharpened my eye for detail, composition, and storytelling in design.

Over the past few years — particularly through June 2025 — I've explored architecture through academic studios, competitions, and self-driven projects. Most recently, I was awarded Second Prize in the 2nd Re-ConD'25 international student competition held in Turkey for my project "Contemporary Art Center," which reimagines contemporary architecture within historical contexts.

Skilled in CAD, Revit, 3ds Max, and Adobe Suite, I enjoy working across scales — from interior and exterior design to urban regeneration. I'm particularly drawn to projects that integrate sustainability, cultural continuity, and adaptive reuse as core design principles.

As a junior architect, I'm excited to expand my professional journey through internships, collaborations, and research opportunities where I can contribute creative, sustainable solutions to today's architectural challenges. Beyond architecture, I continue to practice photography, which fuels my creativity and lets me see the built environment from unique perspectives.

These four photographs explore how architecture can be seen through layers, openings, and frames. Each image uses a different architectural element to reveal another space, creating depth and discovery. Light and shadow guide the eye toward the subject, while the dark framing elements create a consistent visual language — together showing how architecture shapes not only the spaces we occupy, but also the way we see them.
