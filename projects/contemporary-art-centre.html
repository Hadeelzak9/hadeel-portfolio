Architecture Award · June 2025

"Living Ink of Culture" — 2nd RE-ConD'25

Contemporary Art Centre

A Contemporary Art Centre reimagined within the historic urban fabric of Darb Al-Ahmar, Cairo, designed for the 2nd RE-ConD'25 International Student Competition under the theme "Palimpsest." Arabic calligraphy is used as both metaphor and architectural method — reusing and re-evaluating a contemporary design within its historical context.
